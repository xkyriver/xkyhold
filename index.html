What Google can and cannot see

Your backup lives in your Drive, but it is unreadable without your password or recovery key.

Google sees

  • That you have a folder called “XkyHold”
  • How much space it takes
  • When you back up

Google cannot see

  • What your files contain
  • What they are called
  • How your folders are organised

There is no XkyHold server. Your files go from your browser to your Drive and nowhere else. Privacy policy

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no paid features. Your backup uses the space in your Google Drive.

Which browsers can I use?

To make backups: Chrome or Edge on a computer, the browsers that can read your folders. To restore: any modern browser, including Firefox and Safari or Chrome on a phone, which give you your files as a .zip download.

What if I forget my password?

Use your recovery key. Only your password or your recovery key can open your backup. If you lose both, nobody can open it, not even us.

Does it back up while I am away?

No. A backup runs only while the XkyHold page is open, and only when you start it. XkyHold reminds you when one is due.

Does it back up my whole computer?

No, it backs up the folders you choose, such as documents, pictures or work. It does not keep file permissions or shortcuts (symbolic links).

Can I use a work or school account?

Yes, but your administrator may have to approve XkyHold first. What to approve

Can I check how it works?

Yes. The source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence, including the description of how backups are stored. Source code
