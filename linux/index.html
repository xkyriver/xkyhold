Your whole home folder

Documents and pictures, and also settings, keys, permissions and links. Caches are left out for you.

Encrypted on your computer

Google Drive and the disk only ever see encrypted data. Only your password or recovery key opens it.

A reminder, or automatic

By default it reminds you when a backup is due. If you want, it backs up by itself, even with the window closed.

Restore anywhere

One file or everything, to a new folder, a disk or its original place. On a new computer, too.

Install

Download the file for your system from the latest release. For 64-bit Intel and AMD computers.

  1. 1

    Any distribution: AppImage

    Make the file executable, then open it.

    chmod +x xkyhold-lx_*.AppImage
./xkyhold-lx_*.AppImage
  2. 2

    Debian, Ubuntu, Mint: .deb

    Install it, then open XkyHold-lx from your applications.

    sudo apt install ./xkyhold-lx_*.deb
  3. 3

    Fedora, openSUSE: .rpm

    Install it, then open XkyHold-lx from your applications.

    sudo dnf install ./xkyhold-lx-*.rpm

To keep your backup's key on this computer, and for automatic backups, XkyHold-lx uses your desktop's keyring (GNOME Keyring or KWallet). Without one, it asks for your password each time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no paid features. Your backup uses the space in your Google Drive or on your disk.

Does it back up the whole system?

No. It backs up your personal folder only. It does not save the system, installed programs or other users' folders, and it never runs as administrator.

What is left out?

Caches, the trash and the app's own files, always. You can also leave out folders such as Videos, and large folders that are easy to rebuild, such as node_modules. XkyHold-lx shows how much space each one takes.

What if I forget my password?

Use your recovery key. Only your password or your recovery key can open your backup. If you lose both, nobody can open it, not even us.

I lost my computer. How do I get my files back?

Install XkyHold-lx on the new computer, choose the same Google account or disk, and open your backup with its password or recovery key. Without Linux, XkyHold in the browser can restore it too.

Can I use a work or school account?

Yes, but your administrator may have to approve XkyHold-lx first. What to approve

How do I uninstall it?

First open Settings and choose “Remove from this computer”. This turns off reminders and automatic backups and forgets the key; your backup stays where it is. Then uninstall the package, or delete the AppImage.

Can I check how it works?

Yes. The source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence, including the description of how backups are stored. Source code
